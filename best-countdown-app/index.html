Best countdown widget apps for iPhone (2026)

Most countdown apps are free to download. The differences are in what's free after that: the widgets, ads, and whether you end up paying monthly. Here are eight popular ones side by side.

We make Simple Count, one of the apps below, so read this with that in mind. Quick answer

  • Free widgets, no ads, no subscription: Simple Count. Every Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch widget is free, and the only paid option is a $4.99 one-time unlock.
  • Apple Watch: Simple Count (free: watch face complications, Smart Stack and a watch app), Countdown (Find Appiness), Countdown Star or Pretty Progress.
  • Couples with a shared chat: Outside. Its free plan includes sharing and widgets; chat and recurring events are in its Pro subscription.
  • Designing the widget itself (styles, fonts): Countdown Buddy, with 11 widget styles; the free version includes one basic widget.
  • Progress bars and percentages: Pretty Progress shows how much of the time is done, not just what's left. Its free version includes four custom widgets with two designs; the rest is in PRO, a subscription or a lifetime purchase.
  • The biggest, most-reviewed apps: Countdown Star (216,000+ ratings) and Countdown by Find Appiness (188,000+ ratings).

How to choose

Check what the widget costs. A countdown app is mostly its widget. Before you set up ten events, check whether the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are in the free version or behind a paywall.

One-time or monthly? A countdown app doesn't run servers for most of what it does. A one-time unlock (like Simple Count's or Countdown's) usually costs less over a year than a subscription.

Read the privacy label. It's on every App Store page, under App Privacy. "Data Used to Track You" means data is shared for tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

Sharing that stays in sync. If you count down with someone else, look for shared countdowns that update for everyone (Simple Count, Outside), not just sharing a picture or a copy.
